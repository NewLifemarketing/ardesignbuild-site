The A&R Build Process

A clear, step-by-step path from first conversation to final walkthrough — and beyond.

Request a Quote
Before, During and After

Twelve Steps, From First Conversation to Keys in Hand

Building a custom home is one of the biggest projects of your life. It shouldn’t feel like a leap of faith. Here’s exactly how we take you from first conversation to keys in hand — and beyond.

Pre-Consultation

We start by listening. We learn about your vision, needs, lifestyle, budget and timeline.

Site Visit & Evaluation

We visit your property to assess conditions and determine the best location for your new home or cottage.

Custom Design

Bring us your ideas, or let us design a custom home that reflects your lifestyle and fits your lot and budget.

Preliminary Plans & 3D Renderings

We create preliminary drawings, elevations and 3D renderings so you can see your future home come to life.

Planning & Permit Approvals

We assist with planning applications and handle all the permits and inspections, so the process is smooth and stress-free.

Exterior Selections

Choose your exterior finishes with expert guidance in our Design Centre — siding, stone, roofing, windows, doors, trim, decking and more.

Interior Selections

Work with our interior design team to select cabinets, countertops, flooring, lighting, plumbing, millwork and all the finishes that make it yours.

Site Preparation

Lot clearing, blasting, excavation, septic, well, driveway and foundation preparation.

Construction

Our skilled team and trusted trades bring your home to life with careful planning, quality workmanship and consistent communication.

Framing Walk-through

We walk through your home together before drywall to review every detail and confirm layouts, electrical, plumbing, HVAC and upgrades.

Final Walk-through & Possession

We complete a final inspection, review all features, sign the Tarion warranty forms and hand over the keys to your new home or cottage.

Beyond Move-In

We’re here for you after you move in. Every build comes complete with a 7-year Tarion structural warranty, manufacturer’s warranties and our 1-year builder warranty — for complete peace of mind.

Ready to build your dream home or cottage?

Tell us about your project and we’ll reply within two business days. No pressure — just an honest conversation with a builder who listens.
