Design & Plan Customization

Proven Plans, Personal Homes

Our catalogue of 82 Viceroy-style plans gives you a head start most custom builds don’t have: layouts refined over decades, with the kinks already worked out. But the plan you pick is where design starts, not where it ends.

Move walls. Add a wing. Flip the plan to face your view. Vault the ceiling, grow the garage, add a granny suite. Or skip the catalogue entirely and design from a blank page with us.

  • 82 fully customizable plans across four distinct lines
  • Modify any plan — layout, size, orientation, finishes
  • Full from-scratch custom design available
  • Design decisions made on paper, where changes are cheap
Four Lines, One Promise

Every Plan Is a Starting Point

Country Classics

Timeless designs combining a stylish, distinctive roadside elevation with a dramatic, contemporary treatment on the lake or view side.

Browse the line →

Country Retreats

Suited to the cottage-country lifestyle — open-concept plans that maximize indoor space, many with narrow-lot or smaller footprints, adaptable to sloping lots for walkout basements.

Browse the line →

Panoramics

Luxury country homes especially designed for properties with a spectacular view — open interiors, panoramic window walls, and integrated sundecks, porches and all-season rooms.

Browse the line →

Post & Beam

Exposed natural wood timber detailing — decorative Douglas fir and prime painted beams within standard framing to achieve the post-and-beam look without the usual cost and complexity. Open-concept areas with gallery lofts, window nooks, and covered terraces.

Browse the line →
