Custom Home Building

A Home Designed Around How You Actually Live

A custom home isn’t about more square footage — it’s about the right square footage. The kitchen that fits the way your family cooks. The main-floor primary suite that lets you stay for decades. The workshop, the mudroom, the view from the morning-coffee chair.

Whether you’re building your forever home in Simcoe County or a retirement bungalow closer to the grandkids, we design and build around your life — on your lot or one we help you evaluate.

  • Start from any of 82 customizable plans, or design from scratch
  • Bungalows, two-storeys and accessible designs for aging in place
  • Turnkey, shell, or delivered-home build packages
  • One accountable team from design through after-care
Built for Central Ontario

Fifty Winters of Local Experience

Homes here need to handle real winters, spring thaws, and everything a rural lot can throw at a foundation. We’ve been building for this climate since 1973 — the details that matter here are second nature to us.

And because we’re HCRA-licensed with a clean record, you can verify our standing before you ever shake our hand.
