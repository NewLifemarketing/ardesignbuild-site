Four Seasons, or Not

Specifying a building for January rather than July changes insulation, heating, plumbing protection and often the water supply and the road as well. In Georgian Bay it also interacts with access: this is not an area where winter access can be assumed, and a lot that is easy in August can be genuinely difficult in February.

It is not only a question about how you intend to use it now. Buildings in this region change hands and change use, and retrofitting for winter afterwards costs considerably more than specifying it at design stage.
