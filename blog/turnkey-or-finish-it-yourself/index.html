Turnkey Or Finish It Yourself? How Our Build Packages Work

Bronze, Silver, Gold or Platinum — how much of your custom home you want finished changes a quote more than almost anything else. A plain look at what each level covers.

Two builders can quote the same house and come back thousands of dollars apart without either of them being wrong. Usually the difference is not the framing or the foundation. It is how much of the finishing is in the number.

That is the part worth understanding before you compare anything, because it is also the part you get to decide.

We’ll cover:

  • What “finished” actually means
  • The four levels, plainly
  • Where the money actually moves
  • Most people land between two

What “finished” actually means

“Finished” is not one thing. A house can be weathertight, insulated and structurally complete and still have no flooring, no cabinetry and no light fixtures. It can also arrive with every last selection installed and the keys in your hand.

Both are finished houses. They are just finished to different points, and the gap between them is a large share of what a build costs.

So rather than quote one version and hope it matches what you had in mind, we set the level explicitly and write it down.

The four levels, plainly

There are four, and the names matter less than what each one hands you.

Bronze is the structure finished and ready for you, or your own trades, to complete. People choose it when they have the skills, the contacts or the time, and would rather spend them than money.

Silver is a complete, move-in-ready finish with quality standard selections. Nothing is left for you to arrange.

Gold is upgraded finishes and features throughout. It is our most popular level, which tells you something about where most people actually want to land.

Platinum is the premium, fully-finished package with the finest selections.

The structure, the envelope and the systems are built the same way at every level. What changes is how far the finishing goes and how much of it is ours to complete.

Where the money actually moves

When people picture the cost of a house they picture lumber and concrete. Those are real, but they are also the parts with the least room for choice — a floor joist is a floor joist.

The choices live in the finishing. Cabinetry. Flooring. Counters. Tile. Lighting. Plumbing fixtures. Door hardware. Each one has a version that is perfectly good and a version that costs several times as much, and the house works either way.

That is why we walk clients through selections rather than handing over a catalogue. Our model home and design centre exists so you can stand in front of the real thing and see how it looks beside everything else you have chosen, which is not the same as approving a sample online.

Most people land between two

Almost nobody sits squarely on one package. Most people land somewhere between two — the kitchen from one level, the flooring from another, and a few things they simply do not care about held at standard.

That is fine, and it is the reason every agreement is itemized rather than picked off a menu. You should be able to read the quote and see exactly what is in it, which also makes it possible to compare ours against someone else’s honestly.

We have been building in Central Ontario since 1973, and the conversation that saves people the most money is almost always the early one about scope, not the late one about price.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I start at Bronze and finish the rest myself later?

Yes, and people do. Bronze hands you a completed structure ready for you or your own trades to finish. What matters is deciding that early, because it changes how some of the work is staged and what gets roughed in before the walls close.

Which package do most people choose?

Gold is our most popular level — upgraded finishes and features throughout. But most people land somewhere between two levels rather than squarely on one, which is why every agreement is itemized rather than picked off a menu.

Does the package change the structure of the house?

No. The structure, the envelope and the systems are built the same way at every level. What changes is how far the finishing goes and how much of it is ours to complete.

Not sure which level fits? Tell us about the house and how you actually want to live in it, and we will put an itemized number against it. Request a quote and we will get back to you within two business days.
