The A&R Legacy — Building Lifestyle Since 1973

Two Generations, One Standard

Our story began in 1973, when Rene Ouellet Sr. founded A&R Carpentry. As one of Ontario’s largest Viceroy Home dealers and authorized builders, the company earned a reputation for exceptional craftsmanship, honest service and quality construction — and over the years proudly completed more than 1,500 custom homes and cottages across the region.

In 2018, A&R entered its second generation when Rene Ouellet Jr. acquired the family business and rebranded it as A&R Design Build Inc. The name evolved; the values didn’t. The vision simply expanded — beyond construction to a complete design-build experience, bringing design, planning, approvals and construction together under one roof.

Our longstanding location on Highway 400 in Innisfil has become a familiar landmark for generations of homeowners. Over the years, our model homes and Design Centre have welcomed thousands of clients — a place to explore ideas, review plans and select finishes with confidence.

Although the company has grown, our philosophy has never changed. We believe every home should be thoughtfully designed, expertly built and tailored to the people who will live in it. For over 50 years, our family has been building homes — and relationships — that stand the test of time.

Established in 1976, Tarion enforces the Ontario New Home Warranties Plan Act. A&R has been a Registered Builder since 1983, with a strong Ontario New Home Warranty service record — and years of Service Excellence ratings.

HCRA Licensed — #B46716

Ontario’s Home Construction Regulatory Authority licenses the people and companies who build and sell new homes. A&R Design Build Inc. is a licensed Vendor and Builder (Licence #B46716). View our record in the Ontario Builder Directory.

★ Our Homes · Barrie · Fall 2019

“Beach Life” — a Cottage Worth a Magazine Spread

The Fall 2019 Barrie edition of Our Homes ran a multi-page feature, “Beach Life,” on a custom cottage we built in the Tiny Beaches area of Georgian Bay for a Toronto couple, Phil and Elsa.

It’s the kind of story we’re proudest of: they had built with A&R’s Rene Ouellet Jr. once before, years earlier near Wasaga Beach — and when it came time to build their dream place further north, they came back. In the article they recalled “a great experience working with builder Rene Ouellet Jr.”

The details that made the page are the ones we sweat on every build: a no-maintenance composite deck off the main floor, glass railings that keep the Georgian Bay view unobstructed, and a partially covered back deck made for long summer meals.

Story by M. Carolyn Black · Photography by Sandy MacKay · Our Homes, Barrie edition, Fall 2019 (pp. 58–60).
