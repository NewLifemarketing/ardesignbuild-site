Visit The Carlyle — Our Model Home & Design Centre

The best way to know what your home could feel like is to stand inside one we’ve built. Come tour The Carlyle in Innisfil, Ontario.

Plan Your Visit

What You’ll See on a Visit

The Carlyle is a 3,174 sq ft home from our Panoramics line — 3 bedrooms, 3.5 baths, and the kind of open, light-filled great room the line is known for. It’s also our working design centre, where you can browse finishes, compare options, and picture the details of your own build.

  • Spend time in our design room and look through the full range of plans
  • See the quality of A&R finishing up close, from trim to timber
  • Talk your project through with the team — a builder, not a salesperson
  • Ask anything, and start picturing your own build

The Design Centre

A Room Where You Choose Every Detail

Right inside The Carlyle is our design centre — a dedicated selections room where the finishes of your home stop being abstract. Instead of squinting at a swatch on a screen, you see and touch the real thing: run your hand over the flooring, hold a countertop sample up against a cabinet colour, and compare paint tones in the same kind of daylight your rooms will get.

Sit down with the team, work through your choices at your own pace, and walk out knowing exactly how your home will look and feel — long before we pour a foundation.

What you’ll pick out in the room

  • Flooring — hardwood, tile, luxury vinyl and more
  • Tile & backsplashes for kitchens and baths
  • Countertops and work surfaces
  • Cabinetry styles, finishes and hardware
  • Paint colours and interior palettes
  • Lighting, fixtures and the finishing touches
