HST Rebate Information for New Home Builds

Building a new home in Ontario may qualify you for an HST rebate. Here’s the plain-language version — and how A&R helps you navigate it.

The Short Version

When you build a new home or cottage in Ontario, HST applies to much of the project — but government rebate programs can return a meaningful portion of that tax for qualifying owner-built and builder-built homes. The rules depend on how the home will be used (primary residence vs. other uses), its value, and how the build is structured.

How A&R Helps

  • A clear statement of the HST you paid. Our accountant prepares a statement of the accounts showing all the HST paid on your build — exactly what you need to claim the rebate.
  • The official links, not guesswork. We point you to the Canada Revenue Agency’s own pages and the required certifications, so your application follows the current government process.
  • Straight answers. We’re builders, not tax advisors — so we’ll also tell you when a question belongs with your accountant, and help you ask it properly.
