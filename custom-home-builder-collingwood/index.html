Custom Home Builder Collingwood

Let the Town’s Checklist Interview Your Builder

Collingwood asks every new house for proof of registration, complete drawings and certified grading. Those three items tell you more about a builder than any brochure.

Choosing a custom home builder Collingwood homeowners can trust is easier than it sounds, because the Town of Collingwood has already written the interview. Its new house permit page lists what every application must carry, and three of those items are really questions about the builder: is the house registered with Tarion, are the drawings complete enough to pass review the first time, and will the lot be graded to an approved plan that someone certifies at the end?

We are A&R Design Build. We have built custom homes and cottages across Central Ontario since 1973, more than 1,500 of them, and we are based in Innisfil, Ontario. Collingwood sits in Simcoe County, which is one of the regions we serve. This page takes the Town’s checklist one item at a time and shows how we answer each, with records you can check yourself rather than claims you have to take on trust.

What Collingwood Asks For, and What Each Item Tells You

Before an application is even accepted, Collingwood wants a Zoning Certificate. The Town has required one for new houses since January 1, 2022, and it confirms with Planning Services that the house fits the zoning before Building Services reviews the construction. After that, the application itself has to include the documents below. We have grouped them by what they reveal about the people you hire.

A Tarion registration form

A new house in Collingwood goes in with its Tarion paperwork. A builder who cannot produce it is not one the Town will issue a permit to. Ours comes from a registration that has been in place since 1983.

A full set of drawings

Site plan, a floor plan for every level, floor and roof framing plans, four elevations, at least one building section. The Town recommends an architect, engineer or Building Code qualified designer, and asks that a designer’s name, registration and BCIN appear on the sheets.

Energy and mechanical paperwork

An Energy Efficiency Design Summary by the prescriptive or the performance method, a mechanical ventilation form, and heat loss and heat gain calculations with the furnace make and model.

An approved lot grading and drainage plan

The house has to be built to grades someone has approved, and on an infill lot the Town keeps a deposit until those grades are certified.

A septic application where there is no sewer

Where a lot is not on municipal sewer, an on-site sewage system permit application goes in with the house.

Registration: Look It Up Before You Shake Hands

Two public records answer the first question. Ontario’s Home Construction Regulatory Authority licenses the companies that build and sell new homes, and its Ontario Builder Directory lets you search any of them. A&R Design Build Inc. is licensed there as a vendor and builder under licence B46716. Tarion, which administers the Ontario New Home Warranties Plan Act, has had us as a Registered Builder since 1983.

Those records are what stand behind the warranty you get at possession: our own one-year builder warranty, the seven-year Tarion structural warranty, and the manufacturers’ warranties on the products in the house. When you compare new home builders Collingwood buyers are talking to, ask each one for the licence number and search it yourself. It takes two minutes, and any builder worth hiring will expect you to.

We are also members of the Midland, Penetanguishene & District Builders’ Association.

Drawings: Where Collingwood Permits Usually Stall

Collingwood is unusually candid about what slows a permit down. On its new house page, Building Services says incomplete drawings typically cause the majority of all delays, and that revisions can add several weeks or more. A complete application gets a decision in 10 business days; an incomplete one takes more than 20. Zoning review, done by Planning Services as a required applicable law, generally adds three to five business days on top.

That is a design-team question as much as a construction one, and Collingwood custom home builders differ on it more than on anything else. At A&R the person who carries a house from idea to approvals is Laura McAdam, who handles design, planning applications and municipal approvals. We start from our own catalogue of 82 plans or from a blank page, produce preliminary drawings, elevations and 3D renderings so you can see the house before it is committed, and prepare the permit set and the applications ourselves. You see the same drawings the Town sees.

Grading: The Deposit You Only Get Back If the Builder Finishes

The last checklist item is the one that comes due after you have moved in. On an infill lot, Collingwood collects a $3,477 lot grading deposit with the permit and calls it refundable upon grading certification. Its Development Engineering division spells out what that means:

  • a qualified engineer or consultant inspects the finished site against the approved grading plan;
  • they send a Lot Grading Certification and a complete as-built drawing to you and to the Town;
  • the Town reviews them and inspects the property itself;
  • and the deposit is released only once that inspection is approved and the building permit has passed its final inspection.

In plain terms, the deposit rewards a builder who finishes the outside of the job as carefully as the inside. On our builds the site work is run by Rene Ouellet Jr., who owns the company and oversees site preparation, excavation, foundations and project execution himself. Grading and drainage are part of our site preparation scope, not a trade we hand off and forget.

Questions About Building a Custom Home in Collingwood

Is A&R Design Build based in Collingwood?

No. We are based in Innisfil, at our model home and design centre on Commerce Park Drive. Collingwood is in Simcoe County, one of the regions we build across, and we travel to the lot for the site visit and through construction.

What does the Town of Collingwood need from my builder for a new house permit?

The Town’s new house permit page lists a Tarion registration form, a site plan, an approved lot grading and drainage plan, floor plans, framing plans, four elevations, at least one section, a mechanical ventilation form and heat loss and gain calculations, plus a zoning certificate before you apply.

How do I check a builder’s licence before signing?

Ontario’s Home Construction Regulatory Authority publishes the Ontario Builder Directory at obd.hcraontario.ca. A&R Design Build Inc. is listed there as a licensed vendor and builder, licence B46716, and has been a Tarion Registered Builder since 1983.

How long does Collingwood take to issue a new house permit?

The Town lists 10 business days for a complete application and more than 20 for an incomplete one. Its zoning review usually adds three to five business days, and it says incomplete drawings cause the majority of delays.

When do I get the lot grading deposit back?

On an infill lot the Town holds a $3,477 grading deposit. It is released after a qualified engineer or consultant certifies the grading and supplies an as-built drawing, the Town inspects, and the building permit has passed its final inspection.

Can we see a finished A&R home before deciding?

Yes. The Carlyle, a 3,174 sq ft Panoramics home, is our model home in Innisfil. It is open Monday to Friday 9:30 to 4:30 and Saturday 10 to 4, and the design centre is inside it.
