Approvals in Wasaga Beach, and Who Issues Them

Permits go through the Town of Wasaga Beach. Requirements, fees and review times are set locally, so the first move on any new lot is confirming what applies at that address rather than carrying over the last project’s answer.

Whether a lot is on municipal services or a private well and septic system changes the path substantially. On-site sewage serving a single lot under 10,000 litres per day falls under Part 8 of the Ontario Building Code, which sets sizing and clearances — including a minimum 5 metre separation between any structure and a septic bed’s distribution pipes. On a constrained lot, that clearance decides where the building sits.
