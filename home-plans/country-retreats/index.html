Country Retreats

Suited to the cottage-country lifestyle — open-concept plans that maximize indoor space, many with narrow-lot or smaller footprints, adaptable to sloping lots for walkout basements. These are homes we’ve built in this style for families across Central Ontario — and homes we can build, or tailor, for you.
