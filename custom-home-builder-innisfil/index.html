What Home Builders in Innisfil Ontario Budget For Before the First Wall

The Town’s permit package for a new single family dwelling warns that development charges may be added to the permit fees, and the balance is due before the permit is issued. They belong in the budget on day one, not in the last month of planning. The Town of Innisfil publishes them by service area. The chart below sets out the figures for one single detached house from the Town’s pamphlet for developments outside the IDAG agreement, effective April 1, 2026.

Three things to take from it. First, the town-wide figure applies everywhere in Innisfil. It pays toward roads, parks and recreation, fire, police, the library and the rest of the Town’s growth services, and the single largest piece of it is roads and related, at $28,460.

Second, the area charge only applies where the service does. The pamphlet says the area schedules are collected for the services that are available, which is why a lot on a private well and septic system sits at the bottom of the chart. That does not make a rural Innisfil lot cheaper overall: you are paying for the well and the septic bed instead, and we put both in the site work quote.

Third, the chart is not the whole bill. The Town also collects education development charges on behalf of the Simcoe County District School Board ($3,711) and the Simcoe Muskoka Catholic District School Board ($2,372) per dwelling, and the County of Simcoe’s own development charge, which the County’s By-law 6930 sets at $14,975 for a single detached dwelling from January 1, 2026. All of them are indexed, the Town’s each April 1. When we price a custom home in Innisfil, we list these lines as the Town sets them, so you can see what is ours and what is not.

One more detail from the pamphlet is worth knowing if there is an old house on the lot. Demolish it and build within five years of the demolition permit, and the Town gives a credit against the new charge. The credit stays with the property.

A Serviced Lot or a Septic Lot: Two Different Innisfil Builds

The same split that changes the charges also changes the work. Here is how the two kinds of lot diverge before construction starts.

On a serviced lot, the Town’s permit package asks for a lot grading and site servicing plan with a zoning summary, sealed by an Ontario professional engineer or Ontario land surveyor. On a well and septic lot, the septic system is a permit of its own. The Town lists a new on-site sewage system, or changes to an existing one, among the projects that need a building permit.

Innisfil adds one obligation that a lot of buyers find out about late. Under its mandatory septic re-inspection program, the Town inspects systems on properties within 100 metres of the Lake Simcoe shoreline, municipal wells, and the rivers, streams, lakes and ponds of the Lake Simcoe watershed, once every five years. The regular inspection fee is $142. It is a visual surface check, but it means a septic bed near water in Innisfil is designed to be looked at again.

Well drilling, septic design and installation, clearing, grading and the driveway are all part of the site preparation we manage on a build, with Rene Ouellet Jr. overseeing that stage. On an Innisfil septic lot, the bed’s location and the house’s location are worked out together, at the site visit, before the design is final.

On the Lake and in Alcona, a Planning Permit Comes First

If your Innisfil property has frontage on Lake Simcoe or is in Alcona, there is an extra step. The Town’s Community Planning Permit System, approved by Council in June 2025, applies to those residential properties, and the planning permit is issued before the building permit.

The Town sorts applications into three classes. A design that meets every standard is Class 1. Minor relief within set limits is Class 2. Anything beyond that is Class 3, where owners within 120 metres are notified by mail and have 15 days to comment. The Town says it will deem an application complete or incomplete within five business days.

For a custom home, that makes the design brief a planning decision. We read the standards for your Innisfil lot before the preliminary plans, so the house you fall in love with in 3D is one the Town can approve at the class you are expecting. Laura McAdam handles design, planning applications and municipal approvals for our clients, and on a shoreline lot that work starts at the first meeting.

Building a Custom Home in Innisfil: Questions

Where can I meet A&R in Innisfil?

At The Carlyle, our model home and design centre at 1942 Commerce Park Drive, Innisfil. It is open Monday to Friday 9:30 am to 4:30 pm and Saturday 10 am to 4 pm, with other times by appointment. No appointment is needed, though a call to (705) 436-4663 first means the right person is there to walk you through it.

What development charges apply to a new house in Innisfil?

The Town’s pamphlet effective April 1, 2026 sets a town-wide charge of $61,915 for a single detached dwelling, plus an area water and wastewater charge where those services are available. The Town also collects education development charges for the two school boards and the County of Simcoe’s development charge. The rates are indexed every April 1, so confirm them for your lot when you apply.

Do I pay the water and sewer charge on a rural Innisfil lot?

The Town’s pamphlet says the area-specific charges are collected only for the services that are available. A lot on a private well and septic system is still charged the town-wide amount. Ask the Town to confirm which schedule applies to your property before you budget.

My lot is on Lake Simcoe. Is anything different?

Yes. The Town’s Community Planning Permit System applies to residential properties with frontage on Lake Simcoe or in Alcona, so a planning permit comes before the building permit. Properties near the water may also need written clearance from the Lake Simcoe Region Conservation Authority before a building permit application, and a septic system within 100 metres of the shoreline is part of the Town’s five-year re-inspection program.

Do you only build from your own plans?

No. You can start from any of our 82 plans and change it, or bring your own ideas and have us design from a blank page. Either way the design is fitted to your lot and budget.

How long does the Town of Innisfil take to review a building permit?

The Town states that most residential projects are reviewed within 10 business days of a complete submission through Cloudpermit. Planning approvals, conservation authority clearance and the drawings themselves all come before that clock starts, which is why we take care of the approvals as part of the build.
