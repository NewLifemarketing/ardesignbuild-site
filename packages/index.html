Package Differences

Four Finishing Levels — Tailored to You

These are our standard finishing packages. Every one can be mixed, matched and customized to your lot, wish list and budget — the levels below are the starting point for that conversation.

Package

Bronze

Structure finished and ready for you (or your trades) to complete.

Plumbing

Install tubs/showers as needed for drywall only. Rough-in for toilet, sinks and ceramic showers.

Electrical

Interior & exterior lights are keyless bulbs.

Drywall

Reg. ½" drywall with metal corners; ceilings smooth and ready to paint.

Millwork

Carpet-grade wood stairs (ready for carpet).

Floor coverings

Not included at this level

Painting

Not included at this level

Heating & Ventilation

High-efficiency furnace; heat-recovery ventilation (HRV).

Fireplace

Not included at this level

Package

Silver

A complete, move-in-ready finish with quality standard selections.

Plumbing

American Std. toilet, Marina sink, Chateau-series taps, acrylic tub/shower. Optional Mirolin soaker or 3'×3' acrylic tub.

Electrical

Builder-standard light fixtures & plugs.

Drywall

Reg. ½" drywall with metal corners; all ceilings smooth and painted.

Millwork

Oak pickets & railing stained natural; hollow-core paint-grade doors; colonial casing & baseboard; ball knobs. Raywal kitchen per blueprint (mostly veneer doors).

Floor coverings

LVP throughout; carpet on stairs if applicable.

Painting

One colour throughout; trim painted white.

Heating & Ventilation

High-efficiency furnace; HRV.

Fireplace

Not included at this level

Package

Gold

Upgraded finishes and features throughout — our most popular level.

Most Popular
Plumbing

Elongated American Std. toilet, Marina sink, Eva/Bradford taps, acrylic tub/shower. Optional free-standing tub, 30" tile shower, basement rough-in.

Electrical

Higher-standard fixtures — pot lights, dimmer switches, valance lights and shower lights.

Drywall

Reg. ½" drywall with rounded bullnose corners; all ceilings smooth and painted.

Millwork

Oak pickets & railing stained any colour; upgraded doors; lever handles, contemporary casing & baseboard. Raywal kitchen (mostly solid doors) with pot & pan drawers, crown moulding, valances, deeper uppers over the fridge, angled glass corners and a lazy-susan where applicable.

Floor coverings

Laminate throughout; carpet on stairs if applicable.

Painting

Three colour choices throughout; trim painted white.

Heating & Ventilation

High-efficiency furnace; HRV and exhaust fans.

Fireplace

Gas fireplace, framed to the ceiling in drywall, with mantel.

Package

Platinum

Our premium, fully-finished package with the finest selections.

★ Most Complete
Plumbing

Everything in Gold, plus an undermount sink, 8" spread taps, and a custom tile shower with glass door, niche and bench.

Electrical

Everything in Gold, with more pot lights and dimmers, plus 4 USB plugs.

Drywall

Same as Gold.

Millwork

Oak stairs & railing stained any colour; builder's shaker doors; 3½" backband casing; square-rosette levers. Kitchen as Gold; 36" vanities; builder-grade quartz or granite countertops.

Floor coverings

Ceramic tile in bathrooms, laundry, front entry and kitchen; hardwood throughout the remainder.

Painting

Six colour choices throughout; trim painted white.

Heating & Ventilation

Adds a humidifier, exhaust fans with timer, air conditioning and a BBQ gas line.

Fireplace

Gas fireplace, framed to the ceiling in DensShield and veneer stone, with mantel.

Not sure which level fits? Most people land somewhere between two. Tell us about your project and we’ll build an honest, itemized agreement around the finishes that matter most to you — no guesswork, no pressure.
